# Leave Policy

_Sample document for the POC. Northwind Ltd is a fictional company._

## Annual leave
- Full-time employees receive **24 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays.
- New joiners accrue leave pro rata from their start date (2 days per full month worked).
- Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least **2 weeks in advance** for absences longer than 3 days, and at least 2 working days in advance for shorter absences.
- Your line manager approves leave requests. If your manager is away, requests go to their delegate.

## Carrying over leave
- You may carry over up to **5 unused days** into the next year.
- Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**, or they are lost.
- Carry-over above 5 days is only allowed with written HR approval (for example after long project deployments).

## Sick leave
- Employees receive **10 days of paid sick leave** per year.
- Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
- A medical certificate is required for absences longer than **3 consecutive days**.

## Other leave
- **Parental leave:** 26 weeks for the primary caregiver, 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver, both fully paid.
- **Bereavement leave:** up to 5 days for an immediate family member.
- **Study leave:** up to 5 days per year for approved certifications, requested through your manager.

## Probation period
During the first 3 months (probation), annual leave can be taken but requires HR approval in addition to manager approval.
