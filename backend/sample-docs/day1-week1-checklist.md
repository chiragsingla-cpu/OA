# Day-1 & Week-1 Onboarding Checklist

_Sample document for the POC. Northwind Ltd is a fictional company._

## Day 1
- [ ] Collect your laptop and access badge from the IT desk (ground floor, 09:30)
- [ ] Sign in to your company email and set up multi-factor authentication
- [ ] Install the password manager and store your new passwords in it
- [ ] Join the #general and #new-joiners Slack channels
- [ ] Meet your line manager for your welcome 1:1
- [ ] Meet your onboarding buddy and schedule a lunch together
- [ ] Read the Employee Handbook and the Leave Policy

## Week 1
- [ ] Complete the security awareness training (mandatory within 2 weeks)
- [ ] Complete your HR profile: bank details, emergency contact, tax forms
- [ ] Enrol in private health insurance and the pension scheme
- [ ] Get access to the project tools your team uses (Jira, Confluence, Git)
- [ ] Read the BRD for your first project with your buddy
- [ ] Agree your 30/60/90-day goals with your manager
- [ ] Attend the Friday new-joiner welcome session with HR

## Who to contact
- IT problems: it@northwind.example
- HR and payroll: hr@northwind.example
- Your onboarding buddy: assigned in your welcome email
