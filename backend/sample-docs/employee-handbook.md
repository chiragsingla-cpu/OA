# Employee Handbook

_Sample document for the POC. Northwind Ltd is a fictional company._

## Working hours
- Core hours are **10:00 to 16:00**. Standard working time is 40 hours per week.
- Flexible start between 08:00 and 10:00 is allowed as long as core hours are covered.

## Hybrid working
- Employees work from the office at least **2 days per week** (Tuesday and Thursday are team days).
- Remote work from another country requires HR approval and is limited to 20 days per year.

## Equipment
- Every employee receives a laptop, a headset and a monitor for home working.
- IT support: raise a ticket at helpdesk.northwind.example or email it@northwind.example.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Expenses
- Submit expenses within **30 days** in the finance portal, with receipts attached.
- Client travel must be pre-approved by your manager.
- Meal allowance on business travel: up to 40 per day.

## Code of conduct
- Treat colleagues, clients and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.
- Report concerns to your manager, HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline.

## Information security
- Use the company password manager and enable multi-factor authentication on all accounts.
- Never share client data on personal devices or personal cloud storage.
- Complete the mandatory security awareness training within your first 2 weeks.

## Benefits
- Private health insurance from day one.
- Pension contribution: the company matches up to 5% of salary.
- Learning budget: 1,000 per year for courses, books and conferences.
